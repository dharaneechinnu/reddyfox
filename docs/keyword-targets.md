# Keyword & query targets

The real searches and AI-assistant questions this site's content (titles,
descriptions, FAQ, `llms.txt`) is written to answer. Derived from the
services and facts already published on the site (see
`frontend/src/data.js` and `frontend/src/company.js`) — nothing here implies
a claim the business doesn't already make.

Use this list when writing new page copy, FAQ entries, or a future blog
post: does it help answer one of these?

## Local / "near me"

- forex exchange T Nagar Chennai
- currency exchange near Globus T Nagar
- money changer near me Chennai
- RBI authorised money changer Chennai
- best place to exchange currency in Chennai

## Rate / converter intent

- USD to INR exchange rate Chennai
- today's dollar rate Chennai
- currency converter Chennai
- lock forex rate before travel

## Service intent

- Western Union agent T Nagar
- MoneyGram Chennai
- outward remittance Chennai
- send money abroad from Chennai
- prepaid multi-currency forex card Chennai
- student remittance for university fees abroad
- demand draft / SWIFT transfer Chennai forex dealer

## Trust / compliance intent

- is [business] an RBI authorised money changer
- how much cash can I use to buy foreign currency in India
- Liberalised Remittance Scheme (LRS) limit
- documents needed to exchange currency in India

## Notes

- Don't chase a query whose honest answer isn't something the business
  actually offers or knows — see the "no invented claims" rule in
  `frontend/src/data.js` and `company.js`. An inaccurate answer that gets
  quoted by an AI assistant is worse than not ranking for that query at all.
- Revisit this list if the service lineup changes (new service added/removed
  in `data.js`'s `SERVICES`).
